Proreta

Privacy policy

Updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers Proreta, a private command center that reads its owner’s own messages, email and calendar. Only its owner signs in to it, with the owner’s own accounts.

What Proreta collects

  • Email: with the read-only Gmail access that the owner grants at Google’s sign-in, it reads the messages in the owner’s own Gmail accounts, with their headers and labels.
  • Calendar: with read-only Google Calendar access granted the same way, it reads the events on the owner’s own calendars, such as their times, titles, places and guests.
  • Texts and contacts: on the owner’s own computers, it reads the owner’s own text messages and contacts.
  • It can’t send, change or delete mail or calendar events, and it reads nothing else in the Google account.

How it’s used

Only to help the owner keep up with the owner’s own messages, email and calendar, for example by showing them in one place, sorting them and noting what needs an answer.

Who it’s shared with

It’s never sold, used for ads or used to train AI models. What it reads is kept on the owner’s own devices. The owner may let an AI assistant read some of it, only to help with the owner’s own messages.

How long it’s kept

For as long as the owner keeps it on those devices. Removing Proreta’s access in the Google account stops all further reading of email and calendar.

Your choices

The owner can remove its access at any time, under Third-party apps and services in the Google account.

Google user data

Proreta’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
